Given a continuous random variable with density function $f(x)$ and cumulative distribution function $F(x)$, the $pth$ percentile $k$ is given by

$$
F^{-1}\left(\frac{p}{100}\right) = k
$$
A quick mnemonic to avoid confusion is that $F: \text{Coordinate} \to \text{Area}$ while $F^{-1}: \text{Area} \to \text{Coordinate}$
