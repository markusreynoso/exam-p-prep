Take many samples of size $n$ from a population and compute the sample mean of each. These sample means are themselves random variables since the samples were taken at random. As $n \to \infty$, 
$$
\bar{X} \sim N\left(\mu, \frac{\sigma^2}{n}\right)
$$
As a consequence
$$
\sum_{\forall i} X_{i} \sim N\left(n\mu, n\sigma^2\right)
$$
